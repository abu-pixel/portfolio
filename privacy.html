Privacy / Plain language

This site does not collect your information.

No sign-up, no tracking cookies, no analytics script and no form database. Here is exactly what happens when you use this portfolio.

Contact form

The form on the contact page prepares an email in your own mail app. Your browser sends it through your email provider only when you choose to send. This site never receives, stores or forwards the form contents.

Analytics

There is no analytics or advertising script on this site, and no cookies or visitor identifiers are set by its code. The hosting provider may keep standard access logs and show aggregate, non-identifying traffic numbers in its dashboard.

Theme preference

If you switch between light and dark themes, the choice is saved in your browser's local storage under aa-theme. It stays on your device and is never sent to a server.

Third-party resources

Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives a standard request for those files. Links to GitHub, LinkedIn, Figma, YouTube, Google Play and project demos take you to those services, each with its own privacy policy.

Your choices: you can email me directly instead of using the form, block third-party fonts, disable JavaScript, or clear the stored theme preference in your browser settings at any time.

Questions about this note? Use the contact page. This note was last updated in 2026 and may change if the site's behaviour changes.
